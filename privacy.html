Sipwell Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Sipwell is a water intake tracker made by Adarsh Kumar Yadav. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data we collect

None. Sipwell does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

The water entries you log, your daily goal, unit preference and reminder settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are removed when you tap "Erase all history" in Settings or delete the app.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Sipwell schedules local notifications on your device. No server is involved.

Children

Sipwell does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
